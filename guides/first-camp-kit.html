BEGINNER FIELD GUIDE

Build Your First Camp Kit

You do not need a garage full of gear to start camping. Build around shelter, sleep, food, light, water and basic safety first; add comfort gear after you know what you actually use.

1. Shelter

Start with a tent sized for your group, stakes and a rainfly. For car camping, a little extra tent capacity gives you room for gear. Practice setting it up at home before the trip.

2. Sleep system

Bring a sleeping bag appropriate for the expected overnight temperature plus a sleeping pad. The pad is important for both comfort and insulation from the ground.

3. Kitchen & water

A simple kit can be a camp stove and fuel, lighter, pot or pan, utensil, plate or bowl, mug, water container and cooler when needed. Plan easy meals for the first trip.

4. Light & safety

Pack a headlamp or flashlight, extra batteries, first-aid supplies, navigation appropriate to the trip, sun protection, layers and a basic repair kit. Check weather and campground rules before leaving.

5. Keep food wildlife-safe

Store food and scented items according to the rules for your campground, keep camp clean, and never feed wildlife. Requirements can differ by location, especially in bear country.

What can wait?

Fancy camp furniture, elaborate cookware, large power stations and specialty organizers are useful for some campers but are not necessary for trip one. Borrow or rent expensive gear when possible until you know what fits your camping style.

Trailnest rule: buy fewer things, but make the essentials dependable.
