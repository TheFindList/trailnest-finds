Safety Field Guide

Stay Safe.
Stay in Touch.

Going farther from pavement is part of the appeal. It also means planning for the moment when cell service disappears, a tire fails, water runs short, or the route changes.

Communication Comes First

Before heading somewhere remote, tell someone where you plan to go, when you expect to return, and what vehicle you are taking. Download maps for offline use and do not assume a phone will have service once you leave populated areas.

Build Layers of Backup

No single gadget makes a trip safe. A more resilient setup combines communication, navigation, vehicle recovery, first aid, water, lighting and enough supplies to handle an unplanned delay.

Before You Leave

Check weather and fire restrictions, inspect tires including the spare, top off fuel, charge communication devices, carry appropriate first-aid supplies, and know the limitations of both the vehicle and the people traveling in it.

Important: This guide is general trip-planning information, not a substitute for professional training. Follow manufacturer instructions and local regulations.
